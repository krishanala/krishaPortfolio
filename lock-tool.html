Case study locker

Encrypts your "Digitizing forms" case study so it can sit safely in your public GitHub repo. Everything happens in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Use <h3> for headings and <p> for paragraphs.

Screenshots get embedded inside the encrypted content, at the end of the text above. Move each <img> line where you want it and edit its alt text. Don't add private screenshots to your repo as normal image files, because anyone can see those.

Use at least 12 characters. Short or common passwords can be guessed.
